# LIFE EASY – How to Use Guide
*Developed by MAHADI HASAN JEWEL*

## 1. First start
Open **LIFE EASY**. You land on the **Home** screen. Nothing is pre-filled: your own tasks and notes will appear here once you add them.

## 2. Set up your profile (30 seconds)
1. Open the **Persona** tab (bottom bar).
2. Type **Your Name** and your **Weather City** (for example *Khulna*).
3. Choose the assistant **role**, **tone** and **response language** (English or বাংলা).
4. Tap **Save Changes**.

## 2b. Home screen
- **Greeting card:** the dashes show your task progress. Choose **পুরুষ কণ্ঠ** (male) or **নারী কণ্ঠ** (female) voice for spoken replies.
- **Assistant card:** tap the speech bubble to hear the message. Replies to your *voice* questions are read aloud automatically (needs a Text-to-speech voice installed on the phone).
- **আজকের সারাংশ:** tasks done, today's expense and number of notes, from your own data.
- **স্মার্ট শর্টকাট:** AI Notes, Drafts, Explain and Plan open the chat with a ready prompt.

## 3. Tasks
- Open **Tools → Tasks** and tap the add button, or just say / type: `add task buy groceries`.
- Bangla: `নতুন টাস্ক যোগ করো ...`
- On Home, tap the circle next to a task to mark it **done**. The **Progress** card updates automatically.

## 4. Ask the AI
- Type in the box on Home, or open the **Chat** tab.
- Switch on **Deep thinking** for longer, more careful answers.
- Internet connection is required.

## 5. Voice input
1. Tap the glowing **microphone** button.
2. Allow microphone permission the first time.
3. Speak in English or বাংলা (language = *Persona* tab). Tap **Done speaking** when finished.

Voice recognition uses your phone's speech service and needs internet.

## 6. Notes and Income / Expense
- **Tools → Notes**: write personal notes. They stay on your phone.
- **Tools → Ledger**: record income and expenses; totals are shown at the top.

## 7. Weather
The Home screen shows live weather for your city (data from Open-Meteo.com). If it says "Weather unavailable", check your internet and tap *Tap to retry*.

## 8. Tips
- Ask things like: "Summarize this idea", "Translate to Bangla", "Plan my day".
- Do not type passwords or bank details into the AI chat.
