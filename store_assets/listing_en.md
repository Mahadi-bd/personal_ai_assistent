## Full description (max 4000)
LIFE EASY makes everyday life simpler. Keep your tasks, notes and money entries in one clean app and ask an AI assistant whenever you need help – by typing or by voice, in English or বাংলা.

**What you can do**
• Ask the AI assistant questions, get summaries, translations, writing help and plans
• Use your voice: tap the mic and speak English or Bangla
• Add tasks by typing or speaking, e.g. "add task buy groceries"
• See your task progress at a glance
• Write personal notes that stay on your phone
• Track income and expenses with automatic totals
• Check live weather for your city (5-day outlook)
• Customize the assistant: name, role, tone and response language

**Private by design**
Your tasks, notes and money entries are stored on your device only. No ads. No tracking. No account needed.

**Note:** AI chat, voice recognition and weather need an internet connection. AI answers can be wrong – please double-check important information.
