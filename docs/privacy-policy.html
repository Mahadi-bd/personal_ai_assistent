LIFE EASY is a personal assistant app (tasks, notes, income/expense list, weather and an AI chat). This policy explains what happens to your information.

1. Information stored only on your phone

Your tasks, notes, income/expense entries and your app settings (assistant name, tone, language, your name, weather city) are saved only on your device. We do not have an account system and we cannot see this data. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

2. Information sent to other services when you use a feature

FeatureWhat is sentTo whomWhy
AI chatThe text you type or speak into the chat, plus your chosen assistant role, tone and languageGoogle (Gemini API)To generate the answer
Voice input (microphone button)Your voice is processed by the speech-recognition service of your Android phone (usually Google). The app itself does not record or store audio.Google / your phone's speech serviceTo turn speech into text
WeatherThe city name you entered (default: Khulna)Open-Meteo.comTo show the weather. No GPS / precise location is used.

Please do not type passwords, bank details or other very sensitive information into the AI chat.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Open-Meteo's privacy information: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

3. Permissions

  • Internet – needed for AI chat, voice recognition and weather.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – only used when you tap the microphone button. You can deny it and keep typing instead.
  • Vibrate – small haptic feedback.

4. What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your personal data.
  • No collection of your contacts, photos, files or precise location.

5. Children

LIFE EASY is not directed to children under 13. The AI chat may produce incorrect answers, so adult guidance is recommended for younger users.

6. Deleting your data

Delete tasks/notes inside the app, or clear the app data / uninstall the app in Android settings to remove everything stored on your phone. Text sent to the AI service is not linked to an account in this app.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same link with a new "Last updated" date.
